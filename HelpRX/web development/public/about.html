About HelpRX

HelpRX is a non-profit initiative dedicated to bridging the gap between medicine donors and those in need. Our platform ensures safe, secure, and efficient medicine exchange, empowering communities and saving lives across Canada.

  • Verified donors and recipients
  • Secure data and privacy
  • Easy-to-use platform for all ages
  • Support for multiple cities
Our Mission

To make essential medicines accessible to everyone, reduce wastage, and foster a community of care and support.
