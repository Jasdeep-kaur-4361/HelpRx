How HelpRX Works

  1. Sign Up: Create your free account as a donor or needy user.
  2. Find or Donate Medicine: Use the search panel to find available medicines or click "Donate Medicine" to offer surplus medicines.
  3. Connect: The platform matches donors and needy users based on location and medicine availability.
  4. Verify & Exchange: All exchanges are verified for safety. Meet at a safe location or arrange delivery as per guidelines.
  5. Feedback & Support: Share your experience and reach out for help anytime.
Why Use HelpRX?
  • Reduce medicine wastage
  • Help those in need
  • Safe, secure, and easy process
  • Community-driven support
